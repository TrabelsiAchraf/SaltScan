Salt Scan tells you how much salt and sodium is in your food before you eat it. Scan a barcode, see the numbers in a second, and track your daily intake against your own goal.

WHY SODIUM MATTERS
Most of the salt we eat is hidden in packaged food. Too much of it raises blood pressure and strains the heart and kidneys. The WHO recommends staying under 5 g of salt (2,000 mg of sodium) a day; most adults eat almost twice that.
