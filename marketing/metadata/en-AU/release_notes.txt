Your daily journal, now fully editable. Tap your ring to see every portion of the day, change an amount, move it to another day, or swipe it away. Go back to any past day and add what you forgot.

Also in this update:
• Enter a portion in servings (½, 1, 1½…) or type the exact grams, with one-tap shortcuts
• A "Today" section on Home lists what you logged
• Deleting a product from History no longer changes your past days
• Save your salt intake to Apple Health (as sodium; optional, off by default)
• A starting daily goal based on why you watch your salt

Thank you for the feedback that shaped this version. If Salt Scan helps you, a rating helps other people find it.
